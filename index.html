What to do in a dental emergency

Stay calm and take these steps while you get in touch with us.

  1. Call us first

    Phone 856-335-2038 and describe your symptoms. We'll tell you what to do next.

  2. Protect the tooth

    Rinse your mouth gently with warm water. If a tooth is knocked out, hold it by the crown, never the root.

  3. Ease the swelling

    Hold a cold compress against your cheek. Avoid placing aspirin directly on the gum.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about urgent dental treatment at Emergency Dental Service.

What counts as a dental emergency?

Severe toothache, a knocked-out or cracked tooth, swelling in the face or gums, a dental abscess, uncontrolled bleeding and injuries to the mouth all call for urgent dental treatment. If you're unsure, call us and we'll help you decide.

Can I get a same day dentist appointment?

We work to offer same day appointments for urgent cases whenever we can. Call 856-335-2038 as early as possible and tell us your symptoms so we can arrange the quickest visit.

Do you offer after hours or 24 hour dental care?

Yes, our team is set up to help with urgent problems outside regular office hours. Call us and we'll explain the best next step for your situation.

Is emergency dental care affordable?

We believe urgent care should be accessible. We explain the recommended treatment and cost before we begin, so you know what to expect.

What should I do while I wait to be seen?

Rinse gently with warm water, use a cold compress for swelling and avoid very hot, cold or hard foods. If a tooth has been knocked out, keep it moist in milk and bring it with you. If you have trouble breathing or swallowing, go to the nearest emergency room or call 911.
